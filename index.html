        How to play

        Teams and turns

        • Team Human: Human 1 (blue, top-left corner) and Human 2 (red, bottom-right corner).
        • Team AI: AI 1 (yellow, top-right corner) and AI 2 (green, bottom-left corner).
        • Turns go clockwise and alternate teams: Human 1 → AI 1 → Human 2 → AI 2.
        • Teammates work together, but each plays only their own colour and pieces.

        Placing pieces

        • Everyone has the same 21 pieces (1 to 5 squares). Each piece can be played once.
        • Your first piece must cover your starting corner.
        • Every later piece must touch one of your own pieces at a corner, and must not share an edge with your own colour.
        • Touching other colours, along edges or corners, is always allowed. Pieces never overlap and stay on the board.
        • If you cannot place any piece, you are passed automatically for the rest of the game; everyone else carries on.
        • The game ends when nobody can place a piece.

        Scoring

        • −1 point for every square left in your unplayed pieces.
        • +15 points if you place all 21 pieces, and +5 more if the last one was the single square.
        • A team's score is the sum of its two players. The higher team score wins; equal scores are a draw.

        Controls

        • Pick a piece in the tray, then point at the board: the preview is solid when it fits and striped red when it does not.
        • Click to place. On touch screens, tap to preview, then tap the same spot again or press Place.
        • Rotate (R, or Shift+R the other way), Flip (F), Cancel (Esc). Fits (H) marks every spot where the piece fits as it is turned now; Suggest (S) proposes a move.
        • Keyboard: focus the board, move with the arrow keys, Enter to place; [ and ] choose the previous or next piece.

        Online rooms

        • One player creates a room and sends the 6-character code or the invitation link. The other opens the link or types the code.
        • No accounts: each browser gets an anonymous id, so a refresh or reconnect brings you back to your seat.
        • The host's browser runs the AI and checks every move. If the host goes offline, the game waits for them.
